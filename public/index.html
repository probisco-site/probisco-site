Fair questions.

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How much protein is in one cookie?

About 5g per cookie, which adds up to 30g across the jar of six. It is not 30g per cookie.

Is it eggless? Is it vegan?

100% eggless. Not vegan: all the protein comes from plants, but we bake with real butter, so the cookie contains milk.

How much sugar does it contain?

6.2g of total sugars per cookie, of which 5.74g is added sugar. It is a cookie, and we would rather tell you.

Where do you deliver, and what does it cost?

Anywhere in India. Delivery is ₹80 on orders of 1 to 3 jars and free on 4 jars or more. We don't offer cash on delivery yet.

What if some cookies arrive broken?

Our cookies are crunchy and packed snugly, but a few may crack on the way. Broken cookies are still safe and just as tasty, so they are not eligible for return or refund. If a jar arrives with its seal broken, or you receive the wrong or fewer jars, message us within 48 hours with an unboxing video and we will sort it out.
