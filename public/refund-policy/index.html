Refund and cancellation policy

What we replace, what we refund, and how to reach us.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

ProBisCo cookies are a packaged food product. For hygiene and food safety, we can't accept returns of food once it's delivered. This policy explains when we will replace or refund an order.

About broken cookies. Our cookies are crunchy, so a few may crack on the way. A few cracked cookies are normal, are just as safe to eat and taste the same, so they aren't covered. A badly damaged jar is different: we'll replace or refund it (see below).

What we will replace or refund

We will replace the affected jars, or refund them if a replacement isn't possible, if:

  • you received the wrong product;
  • jars are missing from your order;
  • a jar arrived with its seal broken or tampered with;
  • a jar arrived badly damaged: the jar itself is cracked or crushed, or more than half the cookies in it are broken into pieces; or
  • the product reached you after its best-before date.

How to report a problem

  1. Take clear photos of the outer box, the jar and the cookies before throwing anything away. For missing or wrong jars, please record an unboxing video starting from the sealed outer box.
  2. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery by WhatsApp +91 88003 05043 or email probisco30@gmail.com, with your order number, the photos (and video, if any) and a photo of the label showing the batch number.
  3. We'll review it and reply within 2 business days. If approved, we'll ship replacement jars at no cost, or refund the affected jars.

What we can't refund

  • A few cracked or broken cookies in an otherwise fine jar.
  • Change of mind, or taste and texture preferences, once the order has shipped.
  • Opened jars, except where the seal was already broken on arrival.
  • Claims made after 48 hours of delivery, or without photos (or, for missing or wrong jars, an unboxing video).
  • Delays caused by the courier, weather or incorrect address details. See our shipping policy.

Cancelling an order

  • You can cancel for a full refund before the order is dispatched. Message us with your order number as soon as possible.
  • Once an order has shipped, it can't be cancelled.
  • We may cancel an order if a product is out of stock, the pincode can't be served, there was a pricing error, or we suspect fraud. You'll get a full refund.

How refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds go back to your original payment method (UPI, card or netbanking) through Razorpay.
  • We start the refund within 2 business days of approval. Your bank usually credits it within 5 to 7 business days after that.
  • If money was debited but your order wasn't confirmed, the payment is usually reversed automatically within 5 to 7 business days. If it isn't, contact us with the transaction details.
