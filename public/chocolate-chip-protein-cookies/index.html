Ingredients

Soya protein isolate, sugar, butter, refined wheat flour (maida), almond flour, oats flour, chocolate chips, raising agent (baking powder).

Is ProBisCo eggless?

Yes, 100% eggless.

Is it vegan?

No. All the protein comes from plants, but we bake with real butter, so the cookie contains milk.

Does it contain whey?

No. The protein comes from soya protein isolate, almond flour and oats.

How much protein is in one cookie?

About 5g per cookie, which adds up to 30g across the jar of six. It is not 30g per cookie.

How much sugar does it contain?

6.2g of total sugars per cookie, of which 5.74g is added sugar. It is a cookie, and we would rather tell you.

How should I store it?

In a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, with the lid tightly closed. A jar stays fresh for four months from the date of manufacture.

Where do you deliver, and what does it cost?

Anywhere in India. Delivery is ₹80 on orders of 1 to 3 jars and free on 4 jars or more.

What if some cookies arrive broken?

A few cracked cookies are normal for a crunchy cookie and still taste the same. But if a jar arrives badly damaged (the jar is cracked, the seal is broken, or more than half the cookies are crushed), send us photos on WhatsApp or email within 48 hours of delivery and we'll replace that jar or refund it.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Not yet. We accept UPI, cards and netbanking through Razorpay.
